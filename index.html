How it works

  • 👪

    Groups

    Create a group for your family or friends, or join one someone already made with their invite code. You can be part of more than one group.

  • 🎁

    Wishlists

    Inside a group, tap someone's name to see their wishlist. On your own list, tap "+ Add item" — paste a store link and we'll try to grab a photo automatically.

  • 🤫

    It's a surprise

    When someone marks an item as bought for you, that stays hidden on your own list — so you won't spoil your own gift. Everyone else can see what's already been bought.

  • ✏️

    Nicknames & settings

    Open the menu (☰) to set a nickname for a group, switch between light, dark, or system theme, or adjust text size and compact view.

  • 🔑

    Locked out?

    There's no self-serve password reset yet — ask whoever manages this Wishlist to reset it for you from the Admin screen.
